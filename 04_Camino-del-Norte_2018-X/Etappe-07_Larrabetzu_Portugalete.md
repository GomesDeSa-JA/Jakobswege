Schon vor Portugalete hatte ich mir ein Menü des Tages gegönnt. Zum Glück, denn inzwischen war ich so müde, dass ich keinerlei Lust mehr hatte, noch irgendwo nach einer Möglichkeit zum Essen zu suchen. Ich wollte nur noch duschen und mich hinlegen.

Und genau das tat ich auch.

Die etwa 28 Kilometer (?) waren sicherlich nicht die längste Strecke, die ich in meinem Leben gelaufen bin. Aber die Müdigkeit, die mich in diesem Moment überkam, als ich endlich die Albergue erreichte, war etwas Besonderes. Und die Erleichterung darüber, dass tatsächlich noch ein Bett frei war, hat sich tief in meine Erinnerung eingeprägt.

Dieser Augenblick ist bis heute so lebendig geblieben, als wäre es erst gestern gewesen. Ich sehe mich noch dort ankommen – müde, erschöpft und einfach nur glücklich, einen Platz zum Schlafen gefunden zu haben.

---
#### 🇬🇧 That Wasn't the Plan – Bilbao Was My Destination

When I set off that morning, I had a completely different plan. But little by little, all the interesting things along the riverside promenade kept catching my attention. What was supposed to be just a short detour gradually turned into a much longer walk. And at some point, I simply kept going, without thinking too much about it.

When I finally realized that there was no longer any way to make a 90° turn and head back toward the albergue, I decided to continue all the way to Portugalete.

On the way from Bilbao to Portugalete, I had not followed the yellow arrows. Once I reached Portugalete, I crossed the pedestrian bridge and continued walking. At some point I thought: It can't be much farther now. Judging by how long I had already been walking, the albergue had to be very close.

So I switched on the GPS on my phone – only to discover that, once again, I had walked too far.

Luckily, I noticed it in time. Otherwise, I would probably have had to take the flashlight out of my backpack to find my way to the albergue in the dark.

Finally, some good news: there was still a bed available.

Before reaching Portugalete, I had already treated myself to a menú del día. Thank goodness I had, because by then I was so tired that I had absolutely no desire to go looking for somewhere to eat. All I wanted was to take a shower and lie down.

And that is exactly what I did.

The roughly 28 kilometres were certainly not the longest distance I have ever walked. But the exhaustion I felt at that moment, when I finally reached the albergue, was something special. And the relief of discovering that there was still a bed available has stayed deeply etched in my memory.

Even today, I can remember that moment with complete clarity, as if it had happened only yesterday. I can still see myself arriving there – tired, exhausted, and simply happy to have found a place to sleep.
